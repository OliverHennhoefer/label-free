Ma, Zhao, Zhang and Akoglu, SIGKDD Explorations 2023. 297 models from 8 detector families, 39 datasets, one-sided paired Wilcoxon tests. Random is the mean over all 297 models (AP 0.342). The iForest baseline is the mean over 81 randomly configured Isolation Forests (AP 0.399), which ranks like the 84th best model. Cluster indices are Xie–Beni, R-squared and Calinski–Harabasz, which select the same models. IREOS took more than 16 days on the largest dataset.
