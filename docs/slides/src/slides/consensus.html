IdeaErrors are idiosyncratic, the truth is shared. The detector closest to all others is closest to the truth.

MetricsModel centrality (MC), UDR, HITS, ENS.

Fails whenThe pool shares a blind spot, or near-copies of one detector outvote the rest.

Line weight is rank agreement between two detectors, node size the mean agreement with all others. Consensus assumes errors are idiosyncratic and the truth is shared. A random scorer agrees with nobody. MC is the mean rank correlation with all others; UDR restricts it to the same family; HITS weights the top-ranked points; ENS compares against a pseudo ground truth from a greedy ensemble.
