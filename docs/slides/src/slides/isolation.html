Feature-space isolation

Special case: contaminated data

Do high scores fall on isolated points, and do neighbours score alike?

High score on a point with an empty neighbourhood

Scores smooth along the neighbour graph (left) or not (right)

IdeaAnomalies sit alone in feature space, and nearby points should receive similar scores.

MetricsSIREOS, ASI, ASOI, Laplacian score.

Fails whenAnomalies are clustered, local, or only break correlations. Sensitive to feature scaling.

SIREOS weights each point’s kernel similarity to the rest by its score; ASI and ASOI measure how far the flagged group is from the rest. The Laplacian score asks that scores vary slowly along a k-nearest-neighbour graph. All assume anomalies are isolated points.
