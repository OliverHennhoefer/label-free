# Research notes from the initial audit

## Literature coverage

The library covers the major classical families of internal batch criteria. These include score partitions, feature-space separation, graph smoothness, IREOS/SIREOS, EM/MV, consensus and refit stability. It also implements the three AutoUAD criteria and ASI/ASOI. Ma et al. evaluated seven strategy families on 39 tasks and 297 candidate configurations. Their negative result motivates comparisons with random selection and default Isolation Forest. It applies to the strategies, portfolio and tasks they tested. [Library inventory and documented reference-code differences](../docs/metrics.md) · [Ma et al.](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/lakoglu/pubs/23-SigKDDExplor-UnsupervisedModelSelection-WhitePaperSurvey.pdf) · [AutoUAD](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/bf375a8dabbae40def018957ea68842a-Abstract-Conference.html)

For a consensus-based publication, a close additional comparator is Idan's Accurately-Diverse ensemble, which uses exact and fuzzy rank agreement and can evaluate a single detector. LSCP also uses local agreement for ensemble selection. The common ideas of tail agreement, diversity and stability already have prior art. An ablation needs to isolate the proposed contribution. [Idan](https://doi.org/10.3233/FAIA240859) · [LSCP](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.01528) · [Perini et al.](https://lorenzo-perini.github.io/files/RankingStabilityPaper.pdf)

Specify what information each method uses. AutoUAD's NPD requires generated samples and scores on held-out real data. Its paper emphasizes normal training data, while the primary bank fits on naturally contaminated batches. ASOI's published weights were fitted against F1 across labeled datasets. MetaOD, ELECT and LOTUS use historical task performance, and LOTUS specifically evaluates on ADBench with PyOD baselines. AutoAnoEval uses feature semantics and generated pseudo-anomalies. These are relevant comparisons under different information budgets. Check source-dataset overlap before crediting transfer to a new task. [ASOI weighting](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40747-025-02204-0) · [MetaOD](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/23c894276a2c5a16470e6a31f4618d73-Abstract.html) · [ELECT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.01834) · [LOTUS](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10994-025-06984-x) · [AutoAnoEval](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-eacl.183/)

## First iteration

On breastw, Cardio, Ionosphere and Pima, two seeds each and eight PyOD configurations, the example with local-density conditioning achieved macro AP 0.720. Its single-bin ablation reached 0.729. Default Isolation Forest scored 0.705, HITS 0.718, S_Dbw 0.726 and uniform model selection 0.600. The conditioning changed the winning set on only one of eight tasks, Ionosphere at seed 0. Repeated seeds on the same source supply correlated evidence. These are development observations from four sources.

One open consensus question is whether a family-balanced vote on tail overlap is sufficient. The single-bin version is now available as a frozen comparator. A future consensus study could compare it with pooled votes without family balancing, HITS, S_Dbw, centrality and a development-chosen fixed detector. If an advantage appears only in a subset, specify the population before examining more sources. For automatic applicability decisions, evaluate the fallback and coverage on every held-out source. A subgroup discovered from hidden labels is exploratory. [Frozen pooled selector](hypotheses/pooled_agreement.py) · [Research brief and first mechanism-driven experiment](../research/README.md)

For later time-series work, retain the submission and grading interface. Replace the current batch preparation with chronological splits, shared time anchors and blocked perturbations, then add event- or range-aware utility to the grader. TSB-AutoAD already benchmarks model-selection strategies in that setting. [TSB-AutoAD](https://github.com/thedatumorg/TSB-AutoAD)
