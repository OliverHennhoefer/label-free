## Evaluation decision recorded before proposals

Two independent researchers pursue a publishable insight into label-free detector selection. A third investigates rival explanations and prior art. Each investigator owns a separate directory in this folder. The inventors can use the existing four-source development report, the public selector API, synthetic controls and primary literature. They submit one frozen method each, or record a reason the available observations cannot support a choice. The evaluator owns all grading.

The referee reserves six additional, source-disjoint ADBench development datasets, two seeds each, the existing eight-model PyOD portfolio, the 2,000-row cap and the existing public evidence views. The six names are frozen in the referee's allocation before proposals. The inventors do not inspect these datasets or their reports before submission. The referee releases all frozen results together. The bank remains development evidence for later work. Validation and test are reserved.

AP regret is the common endpoint. The general-selection comparisons are default Isolation Forest, IForest/64 (the strongest fixed policy on the four open sources), HITS, S_Dbw, and the frozen pooled-agreement control. Uniform detector choice gives the expected utility of random selection. Report per-source wins and harms, the mean paired AP difference, source-block uncertainty and coverage for each frozen method. A scoped claim can declare another operational endpoint or an observable regime in its prediction, with all-batch results and abstentions retained. A post-hoc winning subset is a question for a new experiment.

Call a method a **lead** if its distinct mechanism survives a discriminating control and its declared prediction transfers to fresh sources against the strongest relevant rival and a strong fixed detector. Retain a negative or ambiguous finding, including any material regression, as an outcome. A six-source cohort can identify a lead or a failure mode. Establishing a breakthrough would additionally require an adequate novelty search and independent confirmation of the frozen claim. One frozen round per inventor is the budget for this cohort.
