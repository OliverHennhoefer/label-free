# Which parts of the first two experiments already have prior art?

**Reviewed 2026-11-21.** This was a targeted search for prior work on conditional feature prediction, deletion effects, unsupervised detector selection and the mass–volume interpretation. Queries included “FRaC feature modeling anomaly detection”, “unsupervised outlier model selection removal prediction” and “anomaly ranking supervised bipartite ranking mass volume”. The sources below support specific claims. This search is insufficient to establish priority for a new selector.

| Primary work | What it establishes | Consequence here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Noto, Brodley and Slonim, FRaC](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22639542/) | Predicting individual features from the others can produce an anomaly score for a row. | Include a matched conditional-residual comparator when a new selector appeals to feature predictability. Our linear comparator isolates that idea and is a simplified model. |
| [Koh and Liang, Understanding Black-box Predictions via Influence Functions](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/koh17a.html) | Changes in fitted predictions under training-point interventions have a substantial data-influence literature. | A future novelty claim would need to isolate the detector-selection contribution and compare with credible influence-based alternatives. |
| [Clémençon and Robbiano, Anomaly Ranking as Supervised Bipartite Ranking](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/clemencon14.html) | For density ordering on compact support, they connect mass–volume curves with ROC curves against a uniform reference. | State which reference distribution and anomaly semantics a proposed decision serves. |
| [Goix, How to Evaluate the Quality of Unsupervised Anomaly Detection Algorithms?](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.01152) | Studies EM/MV criteria and feature subsampling for high-dimensional estimation. | Changing the estimator requires comparison against the exact quantity and the published approximation under common budgets. |
| [Ma et al., The Need for Unsupervised Outlier Model Selection](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/lakoglu/pubs/23-SigKDDExplor-UnsupervisedModelSelection-WhitePaperSurvey.pdf) | Compares internal strategies with random and default Isolation Forest on 39 tasks and 297 candidate configurations. | Retain fixed and random controls, state the detector portfolio, and report the realized utility of the choice. |

**Open prior-art question.** Has a previous method specifically ranked *candidate detectors* by the effect of removing their alert sets on cross-fitted conditional prediction, and in which deployment regime? The pilot supplies little motivation for a general claim. Revisit this question if a discriminating experiment supports a narrower one.
