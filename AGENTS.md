# Research work

For hypothesis generation, benchmark interpretation or a literature review, start with [the research memory](research/README.md). It links open questions, failed ideas, primary sources and the experiment protocol. Record the evidence and decision there when completing a research iteration.

The primary question is how to choose a detector using only known-normal observations. Fit candidates on normal data and select using normal data. A separate hidden mixture of normal observations and outliers supplies the grade after the choice is frozen. The first cohort studied a historical mixed-data setup. Record the track with every result.
